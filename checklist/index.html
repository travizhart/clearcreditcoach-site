Free Credit Report Error Checklist

A printable, section-by-section checklist for reviewing your Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion reports for information that may be inaccurate or incomplete.

Get the checklist by email

Enter your email and we'll send you the free 3-page checklist (PDF).

  • How to get your free reports the official way, and how to spot look-alike sites
  • A checkbox for each bureau on every line: personal information, accounts, collections, inquiries, and public records
  • Plus a few short emails on reading your report and how disputes work

Signups are handled by MailerLite. We'll email you the checklist, a few short educational emails, and news when the app launches. Unsubscribe anytime. See our Privacy Policy.

How to use the checklist

  1. Get your free reports from all three bureaus at AnnualCreditReport.com. Save and print each one.
  2. Go section by section. Tick each line once it looks right on that bureau's report, and circle anything that looks wrong. Our guide to common credit report errors explains what to look for.
  3. Gather proof for anything you circled, like statements or letters from the creditor.
  4. Dispute inaccurate items yourself, for free. Follow the step-by-step dispute guide and use the dispute letter template.
