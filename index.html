FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Clear Credit Coach a credit repair company?

The Clear Credit Coach app is credit education and self-help tools only. It does not dispute items, contact creditors, or contact credit bureaus for you.

1-on-1 coaching is a separate service. Because coaching can include help with disputing inaccurate information, it is offered under a written agreement that follows the federal Credit Repair Organizations Act: no fees before services are performed, and a 3-business-day right to cancel. Learn about coaching.

It will be billed through your Apple App Store or Google Play account, and the price will be shown in the app before you start a trial. You can cancel anytime in your store account settings. Cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged.

Will using the app affect my credit score?

No. The app does not pull your credit. You enter scores you've gotten elsewhere, such as from your bank, card issuer, or a credit monitoring service. Checking your own credit is a soft inquiry and does not affect your scores.

Can you remove negative items or guarantee a higher score?

No. Nobody can legally remove accurate, timely, verifiable information from your credit report, and we don't guarantee any particular result. What we can do is help you understand your reports, spot information that may be inaccurate, and build habits that matter over time.

Can I do this myself for free?

Yes. You can get free credit reports at AnnualCreditReport.com, and you have the right to dispute inaccurate information directly with the credit bureaus at no cost. The app is designed to teach you how.

How is my information protected?

Documents you upload are kept in private storage that only you and your coach can access, and data is encrypted in transit. We don't sell your personal information and we don't show ads. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How does billing for 1-on-1 coaching work?

Coaching is booked and billed on this website, separately from the app. You're never charged before services are performed: you're invoiced only after the work for a given period is complete. Every client gets a written agreement first, and you can cancel within 3 business days of signing at no cost.
