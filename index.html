FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Clear Credit Coach a credit repair company?

No. Clear Credit Coach provides educational content and self-help tools only. We are not a credit repair organization, do not perform credit repair services, and do not contact credit bureaus or creditors on your behalf. We don't guarantee any change to your credit score or report. Not financial or legal advice.

The app teaches how credit reports and scores work and gives you tools to track, organize, and plan. You decide what to do with what you learn.

Do you fix my credit for me?

No. We don't do anything to your credit on your behalf, and we don't contact the credit bureaus or your creditors for you. If you find information on your credit report that you believe is inaccurate, you can dispute it yourself, for free, directly with the credit bureaus (Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion) and with the company that reported it.

Premium includes lessons on how the dispute process works and do-it-yourself dispute letter templates. You decide whether to use them, and you send them yourself. Accurate, timely information can legally stay on a credit report.

Pricing is coming soon. Premium starts with a 2-week free trial, then is billed monthly through the App Store or Google Play. The price will be shown in the app before you start a trial. You can cancel anytime in your store settings; cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged.

How do I cancel Premium?

Premium is managed by Apple or Google, so you cancel in your Apple ID subscription settings or your Google Play subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel your subscription.

Will using the app affect my credit score?

No. The app does not pull your credit. You enter scores you've gotten elsewhere, such as from your bank, card issuer, or a credit monitoring service. Checking your own credit is a soft inquiry and does not affect your scores.

Is the score simulator a prediction of my score?

No. The score simulator is a learning tool. It shows educational estimates of how common credit scoring factors generally work in hypothetical scenarios. Real scores are calculated by scoring models using your full credit reports, vary by model and bureau, and may differ from any estimate.

Can I get my credit reports and dispute errors for free?

Yes. You can get free credit reports at AnnualCreditReport.com, and you have the right to dispute inaccurate information directly with the credit bureaus at no cost. You never need to pay anyone to do this. The app's lessons explain how the process works.

How is my information protected?

Your data is encrypted in transit and protected by access controls so that only you can see what you enter. We don't sell your personal information and we don't show ads. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
