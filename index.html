How it works

Review your reports and handle errors yourself

Clear Credit Coach walks you through the same process anyone can use for free, one step at a time.

  1. Get your free reports

    Download your credit reports from Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion for free at AnnualCreditReport.com.

  2. Learn what's on them

    Plain-English lessons explain each section of your report: accounts, payment history, balances, inquiries, and public records.

  3. Spot errors

    Use our checklist to look for information that may be inaccurate or incomplete, like an account that isn't yours or a wrong balance.

  4. Dispute inaccurate items yourself

    Use our letter templates and step-by-step walkthroughs to dispute inaccurate information yourself, directly with the bureaus.

  5. Track your disputes and keep learning

    Log what you sent and when, note the responses you get, and keep learning how credit works.

Disputing is free. You can do it yourself, and we'll walk you through it. Disputes are for information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete. Accurate, timely information can legally stay on your report, and the outcome of any dispute depends on the bureau's investigation.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Clear Credit Coach a credit repair company?

No. Clear Credit Coach provides educational content and self-help tools only. We are not a credit repair organization, do not perform credit repair services, and do not contact credit bureaus or creditors on your behalf. We don't guarantee any change to your credit score or report. Not financial or legal advice.

The app teaches how credit reports and scores work and gives you tools to track, organize, and plan. You decide what to do with what you learn.

What's in the guide?

The Do-It-Yourself Credit Report Guide is a 48-page PDF workbook plus 10 editable letter templates (Word) and printable dispute tracker worksheets. It covers how credit reports and scores work, how to get your free reports, an annotated sample report, an error-spotting checklist, your dispute rights under the Fair Credit Reporting Act in plain English, step-by-step instructions for disputing with each bureau, what happens after you dispute, and how to spot credit repair scams.

It's $49, a one-time purchase with an instant download after secure checkout on Gumroad. The guide is educational content and self-help tools only: you decide what to do and you send any letters yourself, and it doesn't guarantee any change to your credit score or report. See the guide.

Do you fix my credit for me?

No. We don't do anything to your credit on your behalf, and we don't contact the credit bureaus or your creditors for you. Instead, we teach you how to handle it yourself. If you find information on your credit report that you believe is inaccurate or incomplete, you can dispute it yourself, for free, directly with the credit bureaus (Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion) and with the company that reported it.

The DIY Guide (available now) includes do-it-yourself dispute letter templates and step-by-step instructions, and app Premium will add a guided dispute walkthrough. You decide whether to dispute, you review and edit every letter, and you send it yourself.

Do I need to pay a credit repair company to dispute errors?

No. Under federal law, you have the right to dispute inaccurate or incomplete information directly with the credit bureaus at no cost, and you don't need to hire anyone to do it. Disputing is free. You can do it yourself, and we'll walk you through it.

If you do decide to hire a credit repair company, federal law requires it to give you a written contract and a 3-business-day right to cancel, and it can't charge you until it has performed the services it promised.

What can I dispute?

Disputes are for information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete, such as an account that isn't yours, a payment marked late that was on time, a wrong balance, or an outdated item that should no longer be reported. Accurate, timely information can legally stay on a credit report, and our walkthrough doesn't help you dispute information you know is accurate.

The credit bureaus generally must investigate within 30 days. The outcome depends on their investigation, and we don't guarantee any particular outcome.

Will using the app affect my credit score?

No. The app does not pull your credit. You enter scores you've gotten elsewhere, such as from your bank, card issuer, or a credit monitoring service. Checking your own credit is a soft inquiry and does not affect your scores.

Is the score simulator a prediction of my score?

No. The score simulator is a learning tool. It shows educational estimates of how common credit scoring factors generally work in hypothetical scenarios. Real scores are calculated by scoring models using your full credit reports, vary by model and bureau, and may differ from any estimate.

Can I get my credit reports and dispute errors for free?

Yes. You can get free credit reports at AnnualCreditReport.com, and you have the right to dispute inaccurate information directly with the credit bureaus at no cost. The free version of the app explains how the process works, and Premium adds letter templates and a guided walkthrough.
