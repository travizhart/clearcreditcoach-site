What's included

What coaching can help with

  • A line-by-line walkthrough of your credit reports
  • A personalized action plan for your goal and timeline
  • Help identifying and disputing information that is inaccurate, incomplete, or can't be verified, with your authorization
  • Practical strategies for payment history, utilization, and new credit
  • Guidance on spotting credit scams and protecting your identity
  • Ongoing education so you understand every step

What coaching is not

  • We can't remove accurate, timely, verifiable negative information. No one legally can.
  • We will never ask you to make false statements or to create a "new credit identity."
  • We don't promise a specific score, approval, or timeline.
  • Coaching isn't legal, financial, or tax advice.
Your rights & our terms

Clear terms, protected by federal law

1-on-1 coaching is provided in line with the federal Credit Repair Organizations Act (CROA). In plain terms:

No fees before services are performed

You're never charged in advance. You're invoiced only after the services for a given period have been fully performed, and payments are processed securely online.

3-business-day right to cancel

You may cancel your coaching agreement without penalty or obligation within 3 business days of signing it.

A written agreement first

Before any work begins, you get a written contract that describes the services, the total cost, and the expected timeline, along with a written summary of your consumer credit rights.

No guarantees of results

Results depend on your individual credit history. We don't guarantee any score increase, approval, or removal of any item.

You can do it yourself, free

You have the right to dispute inaccurate information in your credit report directly with the credit bureaus at no cost, and to get free reports at AnnualCreditReport.com.

Honest, accurate disputes only

We only dispute information we have reason to believe is inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable, and only with your permission.

Coaching is separate from the Clear Credit Coach app. The app provides education and tools only and doesn't dispute items or contact creditors or credit bureaus for you. Premium app subscribers may get coaching perks, such as priority booking.
